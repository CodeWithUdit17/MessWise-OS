  • Green Points Gamification: Earn +15 Green Points for advance skips; live points balance badge.
  • Interactive Meal Schedule: Tabbed breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner with calories and allergen tags.

❓ Anticipated Judge Questions & Bulletproof Answers

Q: What prevents students from opting out at the very last minute when food is already cooking?
A: We enforced an algorithmic 3-hour cutoff rule. The app automatically disables opt-out 3 hours prior to meal service. This guarantees the kitchen staff a locked-in, reliable headcount before vegetable chopping and cooking start.
Q: Why will students actively use this app instead of ignoring it?
A: Behavioral economics. Rather than penalizing absences, we reward proactive notifications with Green Points redeemable for canteen items or hostel fee perks. Additionally, students use the app daily to check today's menu and the live crowd meter to avoid long lines.
Q: How does this architecture scale across a large university with 10+ hostel blocks?
A: The Firestore database schema is strictly multi-tenant. Every document (menu, ticket, crowd metric, student) is indexed by hostelBlock and messId. Adding new hostels or mess halls requires only an ID partition with zero code changes.
